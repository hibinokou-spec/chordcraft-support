Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Data Collection

ChordCraft does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All audio recordings and generated data are processed and stored locally on your device only.

2. Microphone Usage

ChordCraft requires microphone access solely for recording musical instrument input. Audio data is processed on-device for pitch detection and is never sent to external servers.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

4. Analytics

ChordCraft does not use any third-party analytics or tracking services.

5. Children's Privacy

ChordCraft does not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us at contact@kou-works.jp
